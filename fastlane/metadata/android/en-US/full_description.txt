Spora is a peer-to-peer VPN for sharing an internet connection with someone you trust. One person shares their connection; another opens an invitation link and connects through it.

Instead of a commercial VPN server, you use a real home internet connection belonging to yourself, a friend, or a family member. Websites see the public IP address of the connection being shared.

<b>Share your internet</b>
Create a link and send it to a family member or friend who needs another route to the web. While sharing is on, they can use your connection from wherever they are. Keep a separate link for each person, and switch each one on and off.

<b>Connect via a friend</b>
Paste the link a friend sent you and switch it on. Spora sets up a VPN on your phone that carries your traffic through your friend's device.

<b>Use your home connection while abroad</b>
Leave a device running Spora at home and connect through it while traveling to access services bound to your home IP address or country. All without configuring a VPN server or router.

<b>Made to get through</b>
Spora is built for connectivity where networks are hostile: censorship, blocking, geo-restrictions. It works when both phones are behind NAT or a firewall, connecting them directly where it can and through a relay where it cannot. The relay forwards traffic it cannot read.

<b>Before you use it</b>
Spora is a tool for connectivity, not for privacy. Whoever shares their connection carries your traffic the way an internet provider would, and the sharing phone keeps a local log of which addresses its connections reach. Share only with people you trust, and connect only through people you trust.

<b>Diagnostics</b>
Spora keeps connection records, an app log and crash reports on your phone. Telemetry, which sends them to the developers automatically to show where and how networks block Spora, is off by default. The records contain network addresses. Spora checks about once a day whether your account requires telemetry (for example, for a promotion you signed up for); if it does, Spora turns it on and tells you. Feedback goes out only when you send it yourself, from an app of your choice, with a diagnostic report attached.
